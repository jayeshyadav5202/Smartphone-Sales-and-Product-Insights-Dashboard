# Smartphone Sales and Product Insights Dashboard
Data Analysis Project using Power BI

Developed a two-page Power BI dashboard to analyze smartphone sales, pricing, ratings, and feature trends, delivering key insights for data-driven decision-making.

INSIGHTS: 
1.	Some brands and models sell much better than others — they are more trusted by customers.
2.	Phones with powerful processors are more expensive — people pay more for better performance.
3.	A few RAM/ROM variants are the most popular — most customers prefer the same storage options.
4.	Discounts are mainly given on only a few models — not all phones get big discounts.
5.	Display size affects sales — mid-size screens sell the best.
6.	Higher-rated phones get more sales — customer ratings influence buying decisions.


FEATURES:
 
 1. Business Problem:
The business wanted clarity on how different smartphone models were performing in terms of sales, pricing, ratings, and discounts. They needed a dashboard to compare models and make better pricing and inventory decisions.

2. Goal:
My goal was to build a two-page Power BI dashboard that summarizes KPIs like total models, average sales price, ratings, discounts, and lets users filter data easily through a filter panel and navigation buttons.

3. Insights:
   a.	Some brands and models sell much better than others — they are more trusted by customers.
   b.	Phones with powerful processors are more expensive — people pay more for better performance.
   c.	A few RAM/ROM variants are the most popular — most customers prefer the same storage options.
   d.	Discounts are mainly given on only a few models — not all phones get big discounts.
   e.	Display size affects sales — mid-size screens sell the best.
   f.	Higher-rated phones get more sales — customer ratings influence buying decisions.

4. Word Done (Key Visuals):
I cleaned the data, created DAX measures, and built visuals for model performance, sales price trends, rating distribution, brand comparison, and discount patterns.

6. Business Impact:
These insights help the business optimize inventory, refine pricing strategy, target the right models for marketing, and make data-driven product decisions. Overall, the dashboard becomes a single view for faster decision-making.
